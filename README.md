# When the team gets hired, the product gets switched off: six AI cases from 2024 to 2026

If you build on an AI tool, one question matters more than the feature list: what happens to your work if the team behind it is hired away? Below are six cases where the product was shut down after the team joined a bigger company. Each one has a primary source from the startup or the acquirer. Dates are as those sources state them.

## The cases

| When | Who joined whom | What was shut | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 2024 to Nov 2025 | Supermaven joined Cursor | Supermaven was sunset Nov 21, 2025. Users were refunded, free autocomplete was kept. | https://supermaven.com/blog/sunsetting-supermaven |
| Aug 13, 2025 | Humanloop team joined Anthropic | Platform sunset Sep 8, 2025 | https://humanloop.com/docs/changelog/2025/08 |
| Feb 25, 2026 | Vercept joined Anthropic | Vy, the cloud computer-use agent, shut Mar 25, 2026 | https://www.anthropic.com/news/acquires-vercept |
| Mar 19, 2026 | Clockwise team joined Salesforce | Product ended Mar 27, 2026. Data deleted, not transferred. | https://getclockwise.com/ |
| Apr 14, 2026 | Hiro joined OpenAI | No new signups, product stops Apr 20, 2026, data deleted May 13 | https://ethanbloch.com/posts/hiro-is-joining-openai |
| Jul 7, 2026 | Bud (formerly Orchids) joined Figma | Both products shut Jul 18. Hosted projects stop being deployed. | https://www.orchids.app/blog/orchids-is-joining-figma |

This is a set of cases I could verify, not a full count of every acquihire. Some other cases had only press coverage for the shutdown and are left out.

## What the dates show

The notice was short. Hiro's gap between announcement and shutdown was about a week. Bud's was eleven days. Clockwise gave eight days. Supermaven gave a year. Your migration plan has to assume the short end.

## My read

This is my opinion, not a finding. When a small AI product is bought for its team, the product is the first thing cut, because the buyer wanted the people. A tool that stores your data, and holds your workflow inside its own app, carries that risk. A tool you can export from and replace in a day carries much less.

## Five checks before you depend on a small AI vendor

1. Can you export all your data in a standard format today, without asking?
2. Is your workflow written down somewhere outside the vendor's app?
3. Does the contract say anything about notice if the company is sold?
4. Is there a second tool you could switch to in a week?
5. If it goes dark on Friday, what stops on Monday?
